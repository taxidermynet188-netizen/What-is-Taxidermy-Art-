How does Taxidermy work?
Professional taxidermy involves carefully preparing a specimen, preserving the usable materials, creating or fitting an appropriate form, and finishing the exterior to achieve a lifelike appearance. Quality workmanship focuses on accurate anatomy, natural positioning, detailed finishing, and durable construction.
